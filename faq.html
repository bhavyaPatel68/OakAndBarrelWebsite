Frequently Asked Questions

The short answers to what people ask most. Anything not covered here, send us a message and we'll answer it.

Visiting the store

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Friday and Saturday, 9:00 AM to midnight. Sunday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We're open every holiday.

Where are you located?

1523 Florida Court, Suite 102, Evansville, Indiana.

Are you open yet?

Not quite. Oak & Barrel Liquors is opening soon at 1523 Florida Court. The opening date will be posted on this site as soon as it's set.

What we carry

Do you sell cigars?

Yes. We carry a selection of cigars, available at the counter.

Do you sell non-alcoholic drinks?

Yes. Along with mixers and sodas, we stock non-alcoholic options for anyone who isn't drinking.

Do you sell ice?

Yes — bagged ice is available in store.

Can I call to ask whether a product is in stock?

Yes, once we're open. Our phone number will be posted on this site before opening day. In the meantime, send us a message through the contact form and we'll get back to you.

Buying & payment

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and Apple Pay.

Do you accept Apple Pay?

Yes, Apple Pay is accepted at the register.

Do I need ID to purchase alcohol?

Yes. You must be 21 or older, and valid photo ID is required for every alcohol or tobacco purchase — no exceptions, regardless of age.

Do you accept returns?

No. All sales are final. We don't accept returns or exchanges.

Do you sell products online?

No. This website is for information only — we don't take orders, process payments, or ship anything. Everything is sold in store.

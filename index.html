Welcome

A liquor store built the old-fashioned way.

Oak & Barrel carries a well-chosen range of beer, wine, and spirits, alongside the seltzers, mixers, non-alcoholic drinks, ice, and cigars that round out a good night in or a good night out. We're a stop-in, ask-a-question, get-a-real-answer kind of store.
